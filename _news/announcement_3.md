XXXVIII Riunione Scientifica Annuale SIEP

---

I will present "A World of Ginis", joint work with Paolo Verme, at the XXXVIII Annual Scientific Meeting of the Italian Society of Public Economics (SIEP).

The conference is hosted by the Department of Economics "Marco Biagi", University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, on 10–11 September 2026, on the theme "Politica fiscale, rappresentanza e partecipazione politica". The keynote lecture is given by Antoine Bozio (Paris School of Economics).
