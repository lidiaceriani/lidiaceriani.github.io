XXXVIII Riunione Scientifica Annuale SIEP

---

I will present "A World of Ginis", joint work with Paolo Verme, at the XXXVIII Annual Scientific Meeting of the Italian Society of Public Economics (Società Italiana di Economia Pubblica, SIEP).

The meeting is hosted by the Department of Economics "Marco Biagi" of the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia on 10–11 September 2026, on the theme _Politica fiscale, rappresentanza e partecipazione politica_ (Fiscal policy, representation and political participation). The keynote lecture is delivered by Antoine Bozio (Paris School of Economics), Director of the Institut des politiques publiques and Professor at the École des hautes études en sciences sociales.
